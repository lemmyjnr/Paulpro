Harbor & Co. — a brand film that outlasted the launch.

Client

Harbor & Co.

Year

2026

Scope

Cinematography, Brand Identity, Consulting

Cover image placeholder — swap in final hero shot

Overview

Harbor & Co. came to us ahead of a national launch with a product people believed in but no footage, identity, or site to back it up. The brief covered a brand film, a refreshed identity, and the site that would carry both. One team, one timeline, no handoffs between vendors.

Challenge

Launch date was fixed and non-negotiable. Every previous attempt at a brand video had come back polished but generic, footage that could belong to any company in the category. The brief was to shoot something that could only be Harbor & Co., on a schedule that left no room for a second take at the whole concept.

Approach

  • 01 Spent two days on location before a camera came out, to find the details worth building the film around.
  • 02 Shot the brand film and the identity photography in the same sessions, so both drew from one visual world.
  • 03 Cut three edits from the same footage: a 90-second hero film, a 15-second cutdown, and a set of social clips.
  • 04 Built the site around the film instead of around a template, so the two never felt like separate projects.

Design Solution

The finished film leads the homepage instead of sitting below a headline. Identity, type, and color were pulled directly from the footage rather than picked separately, so the site and the film read as one piece of work. Every cutdown kept the same color grade and pacing, so the brand looked consistent whether someone saw it on the site, on a billboard, or in a 15-second ad.
